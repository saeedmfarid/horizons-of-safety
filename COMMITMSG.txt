Restructure the manuscript and rebuild both editions

WHAT THIS COMMIT CONTAINS
The manuscript, a Word edition built from it, and a static site
configuration. The markdown under chapters/ is the single source of
truth; Horizons_of_Safety.docx and the MkDocs site are both generated
from it and are never edited by hand.

HOW THE CONTENT WAS IMPROVED

1. Chapter architecture
   The book was written across many sessions, and each session closed
   with its own summary. Chapter 9 ended four separate times, chapter 5
   three times. A reader reaching "Chapter Summary" reasonably assumed
   the chapter was over, then met thirty more pages. Every chapter now
   ends once. The earlier recaps survive but are retitled to the scope
   they actually cover, so "Extended Chapter Summary" became "Summary of
   Quantitative Risk Analysis" and the reader knows where they stand.

2. Section numbering
   Chapters 1 to 7 numbered sections with dots, chapters 8 to 10 with
   hyphens. All of it is dots now. Chapter 7 nested four levels deep
   under a single 7.40 heading whose parent did not exist; those
   subsections were promoted to their own top-level numbers, capping the
   hierarchy at three levels. Every cross-reference in the running text
   was rewritten alongside the numbers, so nothing points at a section
   that no longer exists.

3. Recovered headings
   Forty-three section titles between 7.40.12 and 7.40.17 had been typed
   as ordinary paragraphs with no heading markup. They rendered as body
   text, never reached the table of contents, and left forty-three
   cross-references dangling. They are headings again.

4. Chapter 7 conclusion
   The chapter concluded thirty sections before it ended. Moved to the
   end where it belongs; intervening numbers closed the gap.

5. Duplicate coverage
   Work at height, scaffolding, excavation and lifting each appear twice:
   once briefly as principle, once at length with numbers and stop-work
   thresholds. Both are worth keeping, so the short one is now titled
   "Principles of ..." and closes with a pointer to the quantitative
   section. Four topics remain genuinely duplicated inside the
   quantitative block and are left for a later pass.

6. Editorial residue
   A thirty-line block of reviewer notes addressed to the author, three
   sentences about how the text was being written, and 176 chat export
   timestamps have been removed. What the reviewer notes asked for had
   already been written properly further down.

7. Front and back matter
   A foreword sets out the book's argument and walks through each
   chapter. Closing remarks draw the ten chapters together, thank the
   inspectors and standards committees whose work underlies the text, and
   list further reading in six subject groups. These are the only
   passages not derived from the original manuscript.

8. Table captions
   Three placeholder captions reading "Table 7-xx" now carry real
   numbers, and each Persian caption shares a number with its English
   twin.

TYPESETTING NOTES (Word edition)

Word reads w:jc="right" as "end", and the end of a right-to-left
paragraph is the physical left. Alignment is therefore no longer stated
at all: paragraph direction alone decides it, which means the same thing
in Word and in every other renderer. English companion headings sit in
right-to-left paragraphs with left-to-right runs, so they read correctly
while lining up beneath their Persian siblings.

Formulas are native OMML rather than images, so they stay editable and
cannot be mirrored by the surrounding right-to-left text. Every style
declares a Latin face and a complex-script face separately, so English
inside a Persian sentence does not switch fonts. Each chapter is its own
section with a literal running head, replacing a STYLEREF field that
reported an error on every page not containing the referenced style.

The Persian table of contents builds from outline levels; the English one
builds from a style name. Neither depends on hidden TC fields, which
failed silently on refresh.
